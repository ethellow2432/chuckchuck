About (English)

척척 (Chukchuk) is a to-do app. You type a task in one line, and an AI assistant files it into the right group and tidies up the title. Tasks are shown as group cards and on a calendar, can be shared with others through shared cards, and are kept in a history when finished. Your content is encrypted on your device before it is stored.

When you sign in with Google, the app receives only your email address and account identifier, and uses them solely to sign you in. It does not access any other Google data. See the Privacy Policy.
